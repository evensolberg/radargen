# Radar label collisions: long quadrant names vs the outer ring; ring-name halos at many rings

Long quadrant labels cross the outer ring at 3/9 o'clock and on diagonals (mockup-identical; at N=2 any name over ~80 units crosses r=260). Options: anchor side labels by their inner end, or push out radially by width/2*|cos theta|. Also: with 20+ rings each ring-name halo, drawn just before its name, erases the previous name's descenders — drawing all halos first, then all names, avoids it. From PR 10a reviews.
