# Decide marker-number ink on solid markers (series-6 fails 4.5:1)

White marker numbers on series-6 #2F9626 are 3.81:1, below WCAG 1.4.3 for 9px text; no single ink passes all eight FR-81 entries. Options: a ninth role, per-entry ink, or darker green. Found in PR 04 plan review.
