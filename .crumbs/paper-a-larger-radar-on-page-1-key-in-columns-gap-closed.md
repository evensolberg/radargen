# Paper: a larger radar on page 1 (key in columns, gap closed)

From the PR 21a final review (UI, Important 3). On paper the radar is ~106 mm across on Letter, ~100 mm on A4, ~49% of the page height; the five stacked key rows take ~40 mm, and on A4 a ~56 mm band sits between the radar and the legend. Options: lay the key in 2-3 columns (as the drawing-sized PDF does), wider than the radar's ink; centre the radar between the left margin and the legend; when page 1's legend piece is short, put the key under the legend. Needs a design call: it changes page 1's layout.
